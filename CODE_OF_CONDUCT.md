# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person you are replying to is trying to do something
reasonable, and ask before concluding otherwise.

## What that means here

**Welcome:** direct technical disagreement, strong opinions about design and
architecture, saying you think something is wrong and why.

**Not welcome:** personal attacks, harassment, dismissing someone rather than
their argument, sexualised language or imagery, publishing anyone's private
information, and sustained disruption of a discussion.

The distinction is between the work and the person. "This approach leaks a
storage key" is fine and useful. "You clearly have not read the docs" is not,
even when it is true — say what they missed instead.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and anywhere someone is
representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report to **telvinteum@ubunifucollege.com**. Reports are handled privately.

Consequences scale with what happened: a private word, a public correction,
removal of a comment, or a ban. The maintainers decide, and will say what they
decided and why unless doing so would identify a reporter who wanted privacy.
