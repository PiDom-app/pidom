What Grace can do
Thinking, Fast and Slow
Can read
Open it and read it. Nothing is written back.
Can annotate
Keep passages and write notes on it. Theirs, and you see them.
Can download a copy
Puts the file on their device. Removing access later does not take it back.
Can share it on
Never more than they have themselves.
Narrowing what somebody can do takes effect on their next tap. Widening it does not send them a second notification — they already have the document.
