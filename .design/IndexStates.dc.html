A crash is a state too, and it is not on this chart. chunking and embedding are rewritten to queued at launch, the way an interrupted download is rewritten to paused. The cursor on the row is the last passage that was written, so a book abandoned at page 643 resumes at page 643 rather than at page one.
stale is not an error. An index built by an older model still answers, and is left alone until the reader asks otherwise. A model upgrade writes a second index alongside the first and switches to it when it is complete — nothing is ever half-rebuilt while somebody is searching it.
