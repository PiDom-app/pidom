The reader
React Native owns the controls, the navigation and the reading state. The native renderer owns the page, the zoom and the panning. Nothing crosses that line — in particular there is no second zoom engine over a renderer that already has pinch and double tap.

What is over the page
One activeOverlay, not a boolean per sheet. Nine independent flags meant two sheets could be open at once and every hand-off between them had to be remembered; a single value makes that impossible instead of careful.
Where the page comes from
Documents/library/<profile>/<document>.pdf, straight into the renderer. Convex is never asked for a file to open one, which is why a 600-page textbook opens in airplane mode.
One way to move the page
Contents, find results, the scrubber, the page field and a swipe-to-adjust all call the same function. Five features talking to the renderer directly is five places to fix when a sixth arrives.
goToLocation(loc) The primitive. An outline entry, a find hit, a bookmark and a note all speak one { page } — so the app never grows four ways of saying take me there.
goToPage(n) goToLocation({ page: n }), kept for the callers that only have a number. Clamped, pair-snapped in a spread, announced to assistive tech, recorded as deliberate.
nextPage() / previousPage() One page, or two when two are on screen. What a swipe-to-adjust routes to.
setMode(mode) Remounts the canvas and restores the page. Syncs to the document.
setFit(policy) Offered only in continuous; the other two modes size the page themselves.
toggleBookmark() Marks or unmarks the page on screen. Optimistic, because the icon filling half a second after the tap reads as a miss.
toggleControls() What a tap on the page does, and what pinching in and back out does.
openNavigator(segment) Contents, Bookmarks, Notes or Pages — one sheet, four answers to one question, all landing back on goToLocation.
openSearch() / openPageJump() The find bar over the chrome, and the field that takes a page number.
closeReader() Leaving writes the position, releases the wake lock and returns to portrait.
