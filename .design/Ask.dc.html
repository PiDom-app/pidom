Why does he think small samples mislead people who should know better?
Pidom
Because the intuition that judges a sample never asks how large it is. Kahneman’s argument is that statisticians reading their own results fall for it too — they trust a result from twelve subjects with the confidence a result from twelve hundred would earn, since the mind evaluates how convincing the story is rather than how much evidence produced it. Small samples produce more extreme results, so they tell better stories.
